On my last day of high school, I pretended I was a substitute teacher and taught a class… For whatever reason, I decided to enter a classroom at the last minute. I poked my head in and asked, “Hello class, where’s your teacher?” They all looked at me, stunned that a ‘teacher’ had just walked in. “I’m here to teach today!” I declared, as they nodded in uncertainty. I quickly shuffled to the front of the class and wrote “History” on the whiteboard. “What do you guys want to learn today?” I asked them, which seemed to throw them off, as I had only received blank stares and silence. It was then that I realized I needed to engage with them a little more. So I set down a history book in front of me, opened it up, and started talking about the Civil War. I found a battle that I had read about before and started sharing some fun facts, as a few students started to join in and ask questions. But just as I was really getting into it and the students seemed to be warming up to me, the real teacher strolled into the room, much to my surprise. “Oh!” I exclaimed, “I didn’t realize you were back so soon! The class was just getting interesting!” The teacher looked very confused. The students giggled and, to my surprise, two students applauded. The teacher seemed to shake his head, almost as if he were trying to process what had just happened, and he held up his hand, signaling me to exit. The students were laughing, and I took a dramatic bow and exited. As I left the classroom, I couldn’t help but laugh at what I had just done.
